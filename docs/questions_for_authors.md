# Draft questions for the owner to send (do not send from this project)

To the authors of Evered et al., Nature 622, 268 (2023), and Bluvstein et al., Nature 649, 39 (2026):
1. What was the centre-to-centre spacing of gate sites (x and y) in the 20-atom and 60-atom benchmarks
   of the 2023 paper, and in the dense and "sparse" configurations of ED Fig. 6g of the 2026 paper?
2. Was the pair axis parallel or perpendicular to the row of gate sites, and what was the direction of
   the 8.5 G bias field relative to the array plane and the rows?
3. In ED Fig. 6g (2026), what were the inter-gate durations scanned, and is the fidelity at each
   duration available numerically (with error bars)?
4. During the ~3 us (2023) / ~4 us (2026) recapture periods between benchmark gates, are the tweezers
   at full depth? (This sets how fast an anti-trapped P-state atom leaves its site.)
5. Are the shot-resolved data behind ED Fig. 9 (2023) available, so that the neighbour covariance can be
   compared numerically rather than read from a figure?
