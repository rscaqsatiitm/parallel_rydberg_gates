# D005. Geometry used for K2 (Evered 2023)

Date: 2026-11-20. Task S1. The 2023 paper prints no gate-site pitch and no pair orientation. We use
the 20-atom one-row data set (10 sites), pitch 9 and 11 um (figure-read range, registry
`evered23.site_pitch`, needs human check), and both pair orientations. The K2 number takes the
pitch 9 um and the maximum over orientation and field direction, as D003 requires. A human reading
of the figures (or the owner asking the authors) can narrow this; the questions are listed in
docs/questions_for_authors.md.
